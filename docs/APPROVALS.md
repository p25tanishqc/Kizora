6. Tomato soap name ("Tomato Extract Soap" vs "Tomato Surprise" on the pack)
7. Shower wash name and size (site: 100 ml Shower Wash; bottle: 200 ml Replenishing Shower Gel)
8. About page wording, founder quote and names
9. Customer feedback lines (foot butter, aloe gel) shared as reported by the founder
10. Legal entity line in the footer (Kizora Skincare, a brand of Naturekit LLP)
11. AI crawler policy in robots.txt for the live site (GPTBot currently blocked)
12. Real photos of the unit, the makers and the winter hampers

Product-level items are listed in each product's `approvals` array in `src/data/products.ts` and shown on the product page when the toggle is on.
